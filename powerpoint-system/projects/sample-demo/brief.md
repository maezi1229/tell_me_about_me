# Brief — sample-demo

This project exists to exercise the full engine end-to-end (build → render →
QA → contact sheet) and to demonstrate every layout component. All company
names, people, and numbers below are **fictional**, invented for this demo —
they are not real research and must never be treated as fact in a real deck.

## 1. What is this deck for?
客先提案 (sales proposal) — pitching a workflow-analytics SaaS product to a
mid-size logistics company's operations leadership.

## 2. Audience
- Company: (fictional) Meridian Logistics K.K., Operations division
- Roles: COO, VP Operations, IT Director (3-5 people)
- Prior knowledge: familiar with their own operations, not with our product
- Relationship: prospective client (first formal proposal meeting)
- Their pain point: rising per-shipment handling cost, unclear bottleneck cause
- Their concern: implementation disruption to live operations

## 3. Desired outcome
Get agreement to a 6-week paid pilot in one distribution center.

## 4. Format
- Setting: 対面 (in-person meeting room)
- Time: 20 min presentation + 15 min Q&A
- Target page count: ~9 slides
- Presenter: Sales lead + Solutions Engineer
- Presented live, deck also left behind for internal circulation
